## Currently Building


**[Osmoti](https://www.osmoti.com): your marketing department, on autopilot.**


Business owners can work with Osmo through the web app or send a message through iMessage, choosing the interface that fits how they work. We're bringing demand creation, lead follow-up, and revenue visibility into one connected system.


Our product scope spans:


- **Ads and creative:** campaign planning, branded images and video, campaign management, and performance reporting.
- **Social:** content creation, publishing, and customer conversations.
- **Lead conversion:** capture, qualification, follow-up, and handoff to the business's team.
- **Search visibility:** recommendations for local search and discovery through AI assistants.
- **Measurement and control:** connecting marketing activity to customer and revenue outcomes, with owner-defined approvals and spending limits.


Our focus now is making these workflows work together reliably for real businesses. Capabilities depend on the customer's connected accounts and available integrations.


One early customer, Dynamic 3 Medical Spa, recorded **$6,500 in booked revenue from $360 in ad spend over 14 days**, checked against its booking-software export. This is one customer's result, not a typical outcome. [Read the case study](https://www.osmoti.com/case-study.html).
